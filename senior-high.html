KCC Senior High School has both Academic and TVL tracks so students can follow the path that fits their interests and goals. Every track includes Catholic values formation and activities that develop the whole person.

For students pursuing sciences, engineering, technology, mathematics, and related fields. Focuses on advanced math, sciences, and research.

College Paths

  • Applied Economics
  • Principles of Marketing

College Paths

  • Accountancy
